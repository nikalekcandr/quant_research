Rules

  • D01 Hourly reversal. at HH:00 take the opposite side of the last hour's return; hold 60 min. short-term reversal (Jegadeesh 1990; for crypto hourly: R4c in research.md)
  • D02 Long outside US hours. long at 16:00 ET on NYSE days, exit at the next 09:30 ET open. overnight drift (Lou, Polk & Skouras 2019); R2 in research.md
  • D03 Short during US hours. short at 09:30 ET on NYSE days, exit at 16:00 ET. mirror of D02 (US-session returns were negative after the ETF launch)
  • D04 Fade extreme funding. 30 min before each Binance funding: predicted rate > 0.015% → short, < 0.005% → long; hold 8 h. crowded-positioning reversal (He, Manela, Ross & von Wachter 2022)
  • D05 Fade the perpetual premium. HH:30: z-score of the Binance premium index (last 7 days, hourly) > 2 → short, < −2 → long; hold 60 min. basis mean reversion
  • D06 Order-book imbalance. every minute: Binance spot depth within 10 bp, (bid−ask)/(bid+ask) > 0.4 → long, < −0.4 → short; hold 5 min; one position at a time. order-flow imbalance (Cont, Kukanov & Stoikov 2014)
  • D07 Taker-flow momentum. every 5 min: share of taker buys in the last 5 closed minutes > 0.6 → long, < 0.4 → short; hold 15 min. trade-flow persistence
  • D08 Coinbase premium momentum. every 15 min: change over 60 min of Coinbase BTC-USD / (Binance BTC-USDT × USDT-USD) > +5 bp → long 60 min, < −5 bp → short. US spot demand ('Coinbase premium')
  • D09 Coinbase → Binance lead-lag (15 s). every 15 s: Coinbase BTC-USD moved more than Binance BTC-USDT over the last 15 s by > 3 bp → bet Binance follows for 60 s (run by the fast loop). price discovery across venues (Makarov & Schoar 2020)
  • D10 Open interest × price. HH:01: open interest up > 1% in the last hour → follow the hour's price move; hold 60 min. leverage build-up confirms trends
  • D11 Fade retail positioning. every 4 h: z-score (last 7 days, hourly) of Binance's global long/short account ratio > 1.5 → short, < −1.5 → long; hold 4 h. retail crowding is contrarian
